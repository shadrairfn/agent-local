# 📰 Lead-3 Sentence Extractor Baseline

## Rationale
In journalism and formal essay writing, authors follow the **Inverted Pyramid** structure: critical facts (Who, What, Where, When) appear in the opening 1-3 sentences.
- Lead-3 produces competitive ROUGE scores in under 5 seconds with zero GPU requirements.
- Excellent for establishing a reliable Day-1 submission baseline in NLG hackathons.
